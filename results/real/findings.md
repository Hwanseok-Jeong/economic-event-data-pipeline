# Observed results

Question: how do cash indices respond around economic announcements, accounting for which markets are open?

## Illustrative U.S. CPI (MoM) releases

- HSI: mean -0.797%, median -0.543%, n=3; closed_at_release; mean elapsed since release 37.3h.
- NDX: mean +0.336%, median -0.121%, n=3; closed_at_release; mean elapsed since release 2.0h.
- STOXX50E: mean -0.184%, median -0.134%, n=3; open_at_release; mean elapsed since release 1.5h.

These are nominal +1h targets after the release for open markets or after the next segment opening for closed markets. They are not simultaneous responses. Reopening returns use a pre-release observed close and include overnight information. Only three CPI releases are available; there is no significance or causal claim. CPI, core CPI and jobless claims can be released together. The mean/median and observation counts are in the CSVs. Closed targets remain absent rather than flat-filled.
